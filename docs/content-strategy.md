# Content Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines the communication strategy for Builder Journal.

Its purpose is to ensure every piece of copy communicates the same identity, values, and message throughout the portfolio.

This is the source of truth for all written content.

If implementation decisions conflict with this document, this document takes precedence.

---

# Communication Goal

Builder Journal should leave visitors understanding the builder before the engineer.

The portfolio is not intended to impress through technology.

It is intended to build trust by showing how I think, make decisions, and grow through building products.

---

# Core Message

I build products by understanding people before writing code.

Engineering is not simply about building software.

It is about understanding problems, making thoughtful decisions, and continuously learning through the process.

---

# Brand Position

I am a software engineer transitioning into AI engineering.

What differentiates me is not my ability to generate code.

It is my ability to:

- Understand messy problems.
- Think like a product builder.
- Make thoughtful engineering decisions.
- Learn from mistakes.
- Collaborate effectively with AI rather than relying on it blindly.

---

# My Principles

Every section of the portfolio should reinforce these principles.

- Understanding before action.
- People before technology.
- Judgment over capability.
- Learning over ego.
- Every setback carries a lesson.

---

# Voice

The writing should feel:

- Human
- Honest
- Thoughtful
- Calm
- Curious
- Confident without arrogance

Avoid:

- Marketing language
- Buzzwords
- Overused portfolio phrases
- Empty claims
- Corporate tone

---

# Writing Guidelines

Prefer:

- Short paragraphs.
- Simple language.
- Concrete statements.
- Reflection over self-promotion.
- Stories over feature lists.
- Decisions over achievements.

Every sentence should earn its place.

---

# Hero Strategy

The hero should answer:

1. Who am I?
2. Why did I become an engineer?
3. Why does that matter to the visitor?

The goal is to create curiosity rather than explain everything.

Visitors should want to continue reading.

---

# Journey Strategy

The journey section should explain:

- Why engineering became meaningful to me.
- What shaped how I think.
- The lessons that continue to guide my work.

The focus is not chronology.

The focus is transformation.

---

# Project Strategy

Projects should communicate:

- The problem.
- Why it mattered.
- The decisions I made.
- Tradeoffs.
- Lessons learned.
- Outcome.

Features should support the story, not replace it.

---

# AI Strategy

AI is presented as a development partner.

The portfolio should demonstrate:

- Prompt design.
- Critical review.
- Engineering judgment.
- Verification.
- Reflection.

The goal is not to show that I use AI.

The goal is to show how I think while using AI.

---

# Calls to Action

Every call to action should feel like an invitation rather than a sales pitch.

Examples:

- Explore my work.
- Read the story behind the project.
- See how I approached this problem.

Avoid:

- Hire me.
- Let's build together.
- Contact me today.

---

# Definition of Success

A visitor should leave thinking:

"I understand who she is."

rather than

"I know which frameworks she uses."

If someone remembers my thinking more than my technology stack, this document has succeeded.
